Vision, Mission & Value

The administrative framework, vision, and mission of Banteay Meanchey Province in northwestern Cambodia align with the Royal Government of Cambodia's national strategic goals—specifically supporting the Pentagonal Strategy and the national vision of reaching upper-middle-income status by 2030 and high-income status by 2050.

Mission

is a province located in the far northwest of Cambodia. It shares an international border with Thailand to the west and north, and is bordered by the Cambodian provinces of Oddar Meanchey, Siem Reap, and Battambang. Its dual administrative capitals and largest urban centers are Serei Saophoan (Sisophon) and the major border hub of Poipet.

Goals

  • Maximize the economic potential of the Poipet international gateway and local Special Economic Zones (SEZs).
  • Improve connectivity between isolated farming communities, local towns, and major economic corridors.
  • Shift the province away from basic subsistence farming toward high-value, market-driven agricultural production.
  • Eradicate remaining legacy landmines and unexploded ordnance (UXO) across historical conflict pockets, particularly in districts like Thmar Puok.
  • Sustain high public health benchmarks—such as regional Open Defecation Free (ODF) milestones—and secure equitable access to clean drinking water.
  • Safeguard vulnerable groups, including low-income households, aging populations, and persons with disabilities.
